# Prompt — Mockup Visual Home Page | Antonio Aguiar Advogado

> Use este prompt em ferramentas de geração de imagem (Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Ideogram, Leonardo AI).
> Objetivo: gerar uma prévia visual da home page para aprovação do cliente antes do desenvolvimento.

---

## Prompt Principal (EN — melhor desempenho em geradores de imagem)

```
A professional law firm website homepage mockup for a Brazilian banking attorney named "Antonio Aguiar — Advogado". Full-page desktop UI design screenshot, ultra-detailed, pixel-perfect layout.

REAL CLIENT ASSETS:
- Use the real available Antonio Aguiar assets as visual references whenever possible.
- Hero portrait should be based on the formal seated portrait from public/images/antonioaguiar/portraits/post_04.jpg: young Brazilian male attorney, dark hair, light mustache/goatee, dark suit, white shirt, burgundy tie, brown leather chair.
- About/office image should be based on public/images/antonioaguiar/office/post_02.jpg: petrol-green office wall, gold shield/logo, chair, office shelf/plant details.
- Authority/content cards should be based on the retained real feed assets: public/images/antonioaguiar/social/post_03.jpg, scripts/output_antonioaguiar/images/post_01.jpg, scripts/output_antonioaguiar/images/post_05.jpg and scripts/output_antonioaguiar/images/post_08.jpg. Use them only as visual references for the client's dark/gold editorial language and professional office imagery.
- If the AI generator cannot preserve the exact original assets cleanly, edit/adapt the images to match the premium website proposal while keeping the client's real face, office identity, palette and brand direction recognizable.

UI/UX QUALITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Prioritize a real scrollable desktop website rhythm, not a compressed poster trying to show every section at the same visual weight.
- Keep section spacing generous: 96–140px vertical padding for major sections, at least 48–64px between section title/subtitle and content, and 32px+ between cards.
- Preserve typographic hierarchy:
  - H1 hero: largest type, 64–76px desktop, elegant serif, 1.05–1.12 line-height.
  - H2 section titles: clearly smaller than H1, 36–48px, consistent across sections.
  - H3/card titles: 16–20px, uppercase/sans or small serif depending on context.
  - Body text: 15–17px, relaxed 1.6 line-height.
  - Microcopy/labels: 11–13px uppercase, used sparingly.
- Avoid making all section titles visually equal to the hero; the hero must dominate.
- Avoid cramped text blocks. Limit body paragraphs to comfortable line lengths and add whitespace before/after CTAs.
- Use smooth transitions between sections: dark-to-petrol gradients, subtle gold hairlines, and soft texture fades instead of hard horizontal cuts.
- Never stack section title, subtitle, cards and next section with no breathing room.
- Cards must have stable dimensions, balanced internal padding, and consistent spacing.
- The authority/content cards from Instagram should support the proposal, not overpower the page. Use fewer/larger cards if needed instead of five tiny cramped thumbnails.
- FAQ rows need comfortable height and readable line-height; do not compress accordion rows.
- The locations/map section should have enough vertical space; address cards and map should not feel squeezed.
- The gold CTA section should feel like a deliberate visual climax with top/bottom padding, not a thin banner.
- Footer text may be compact, but must remain readable and separated into clear columns.

COLOR PALETTE:

- Text: white and gold only
- No blue, no red, no bright colors

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Headlines: elegant serif font (like Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond), gold color
- Body: modern sans-serif (Montserrat), white, small size
- Uppercase letter-spacing for labels and subheadings

LOGO: A stylized letter "A" monogram inside a shield with an integrated tree/branch motif, drawn in gold line-art on dark background. Located top-left of the navbar.

LAYOUT SECTIONS (top to bottom):

[NAVBAR]
- Logo left: gold shield "A" monogram + "ANTONIO AGUIAR" in spaced uppercase serif + "ADVOGADO" below in smaller gold
- Nav links center/right: Início | Atuação | Sobre | Contatos
- CTA button right: "Falar com Advogado" in gold outlined button

[HERO SECTION — full viewport height]
- Background: dark petrol-green (#1a2e2a) with subtle gold geometric texture or grain overlay
- Left side: large serif headline in 2 lines, gold color:
  "Seu banco agiu contra você."
  "Existe solução jurídica."
- Subheadline below in white, Montserrat: "Revisão de contratos, conta bloqueada, negativação indevida, busca e apreensão de veículo e suspensão de CNH. Defendo seus direitos com estratégia."
- Two CTA buttons: [Solicitar Atendimento — gold filled] [Ver Atuação — gold outlined]
- Right side: professional portrait photo of a man in dark suit, standing confidently, overlapping a gold accent bar, slight vignette at edges
- Bottom: subtle gold-to-transparent divider with generous vertical breathing room before the next section

[PROBLEMS SECTION — dark background with soft transition from hero]
- Section title in serif gold centered: "Você está passando por isso?"
- 5 cards in a row, each with: gold icon + bold white title + short white description
  Card 1: shield icon — "Conta Bloqueada" — "Banco bloqueou sua conta sem aviso ou justificativa?"
  Card 2: document icon — "Contrato Abusivo" — "Parcelas altas, juros escondidos, cláusulas ilegais?"
  Card 3: car icon — "Busca e Apreensão" — "Veículo ameaçado ou já tomado pelo banco?"
  Card 4: badge icon — "Nome Negativado" — "Negativação indevida travando seu crédito?"
  Card 5: key icon — "CNH Suspensa" — "CNH suspensa por dívida bancária?"
- Cards: dark petrol-green background, thin gold border, gold icon, white text
- Add generous top/bottom padding, more space between cards, and a faint radial light/texture behind the section to avoid abrupt block changes

[ABOUT SECTION — split layout]
- Left: dark background with atmospheric photo of a law office with dark petrol-green wall, gold "A" shield logo mounted on wall, leather armchair visible
- Right: serif gold section label "Sobre o Advogado", large serif white headline "Experiência em direito bancário para quem produz e precisa de resultado", body text in Montserrat white describing the attorney, OAB badge "OAB 6589 — AC" in gold pill shape
```

## Referências Visuais para Briefing Humano

Caso prefira encaminhar para um designer em vez de gerador de IA, os pontos-chave são:

| Elemento | Decisão |
|---|---|
| Cor de fundo | Preto profundo + verde-petróleo escuro |
| Cor de destaque | Dourado (#C9A84C) |
| Fonte título | Serifada elegante (Playfair Display ou Cormorant) |
| Fonte corpo | Montserrat SemiBold |
| Logo | Monograma "A" em escudo com árvore, line-art dourado |
| Tom geral | Premium, sério, confiável — sem cores vibrantes |
| Hero | Foto do advogado em terno escuro + headline impactante |
| CTA principal | Falar pelo WhatsApp (botão dourado) |
| Seções obrigatórias | Hero → Problemas → Sobre → Como Funciona → Depoimentos → FAQ → Endereços + Mapa → CTA → Footer |
